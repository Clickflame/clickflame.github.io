The Problem

We audit a lot of agency websites. The worst part is always the same. The people selling fast websites have slow ones.

Most local customers find a business on a phone, on a weak signal. So we held our own site to the test we give everyone else, and we published every score, ours included.

What We Did

  • We build plain pages by hand, with no page builder and no stack of plugins.
  • Images are cut to the size each screen needs. Fonts sit on our own server so text does not jump.
  • Analytics waits until the page is ready. The lower parts of a long page load later.
  • Every new page gets tested on a phone before it goes live.

The Measured Result

On November 9, 2026 we ran Google Lighthouse 13.5.0 on 47 home pages from one computer in Long Beach. Three runs on a simulated phone each, middle score kept.

Clickflame scored 97. Our 15 sites had a middle score of 96. The 19 top national agencies scored 72. The 13 Los Angeles and Long Beach agencies scored 68.

14 of our 15 sites scored 90 or better. So did 3 of the 19 national agencies and 2 of the 13 local ones.

Our pages weighed 0.4 MB at the middle. Theirs weighed 2.2 MB and 1.1 MB. One of our own sites scored 89, so it is on the fix list. Three agencies beat or matched us, and their names are in the table.

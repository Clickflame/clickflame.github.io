The Problem

RemediationX is a certified water, mold and fire restoration firm in the Las Vegas Valley. Its old site was a slow WordPress build from a restoration marketing vendor.

A water call is decided in minutes. A slow page and a missed call both send the job to the next company. And a rebuild done wrong can wipe out every page Google already knows.

What We Did

  • We built the new site by hand and moved it over on November 1, 2026.
  • We mapped all 306 old addresses so each one still opens a real page, then checked every one after the move.
  • We audited about 45 listings, flagged the doubles and wrong numbers, and added the ones that were missing.
  • A missed call now gets a text back from the number the person dialed, and the leads land in a restoration version of Shop Desk.

The Measured Result

All 306 old addresses still open a real page after the move.

On November 9, 2026, eight days after launch, the home page scored 96 out of 100 for phone speed on Google Lighthouse, the middle of three runs.

The site is days old. Calls and rankings take longer than that to mean anything, so we will not print them until they do.

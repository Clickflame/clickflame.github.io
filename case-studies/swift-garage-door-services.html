Garage Doors, Las Vegas Valley, Nevada

Swift Garage Door Services Case Study On A 96 Page Build

96Pages Built
56ZIP Codes On The Service Map
94Phone Speed Score, November 9, 2026

The Problem

A broken garage door is an urgent, local job. People search by their own neighborhood and by the brand name on the opener. A five page site answers none of that.

Swift needed a site built from zero for the Las Vegas Valley, plus a way to run the leads once they came in.

What We Did

  • We built 96 pages by hand, with 21 community pages so each part of the valley has its own.
  • We added a live service map with 56 ZIP codes, so a visitor knows in one look if Swift comes to them.
  • We set Swift up on its own Shop Desk for jobs, texts and leads. The owner gets lead alerts day and night.
  • Real job photos go onto the neighborhood pages as the crew takes them.

The Measured Result

On November 9, 2026 we ran Google Lighthouse on the home page three times on a simulated phone and kept the middle score. Swift scored 94 out of 100.

The 19 national agency sites we tested the same day had a middle score of 72. Speed does not rank a site by itself, but a slow phone page loses the caller before the call button shows.

Call and click numbers for this build are not published yet, so we do not print any here.

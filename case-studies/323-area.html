Local Directory, Hollywood And Los Angeles County, California

323 Area Case Study On A Photo Led Local Directory

1,649Local Businesses Listed
5Towns Covered
94Phone Speed Score, November 9, 2026

The Problem

Most local directories look the same. A logo, a search box and a wall of names pulled from a database. Nothing on them is new, so nobody has a reason to stay.

323 Area covers Hollywood and four more Los Angeles County towns. It needed to feel like the street, not like a spreadsheet.

What We Did

  • In October 2026 we rebuilt it around photos, with Hollywood first and then Venice Beach, Montebello, South Gate and Westmont.
  • I walked Hollywood Boulevard and shot the storefronts myself. Those photos are on the home page and on the listings they match. You can not get them from a map listing.
  • We cleaned the list before it went live. Spam names, wrong area phones and places that are not a business came out.
  • We kept it light and plain so it loads fast on a phone.

The Measured Result

The live site lists 1,649 local businesses across five towns, read from the home page on November 11, 2026.

On November 9, 2026 the home page scored 94 out of 100 for phone speed, the middle of three Lighthouse runs.

The site is new. We have no long run of clicks to show yet, so this page stops at what we can prove today.

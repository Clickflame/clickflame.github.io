New York Intelligence Agency Case Study On Doubling Google Clicks

518Google Clicks, October 1 To 27, 2026
234Same 27 Days In 2025
249Tracked Calls, October 1 To 30, 2026

The Problem

The agency is led by an Emmy winning investigator. Almost nobody searching for a private investigator in Manhattan knew that, because the old site buried the proof and did not show up where people were looking.

People search this trade three ways. By case type, by place, and by both at once. Most investigator sites have a home page and a contact form. That covers none of the three.

What We Did

  • We built a page for every case type, written for the attorney or spouse who reads before calling.
  • We built a page for every borough, plus the nearby counties where the agency takes work.
  • We built the pages where place and case type meet. That is more than 600 pages in all.
  • We moved the credentials to the top of the page and marked them up so search engines read them as facts.

The Measured Result

From October 1 to 27, 2026, Search Console counted 518 clicks from Google. The same 27 days in 2025 brought 234. That is more than double.

The tracked lines took 249 calls from October 1 to 30, 2026, the most in any month of 2026. Of those, 155 were first time callers.

It was not a straight line. Calls fell to 125 in July before they came back. We say that out loud because a chart with no dips is usually a chart with something left out.

Analytics also counted 1,044 visits from organic search from September 29 to October 28, 2026.

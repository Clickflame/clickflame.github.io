Emergency Septic And Sewer Case Study On Reaching The Map Pack

#1Map Pack Spot, October 26, 2026
16,794Google Impressions, October 1 To 27, 2026
180+City And Service Pages

The Problem

This is a real shop with real trucks. It also had a quiet phone.

A septic company in Georgia will drive to about 40 cities and sells about 6 services in each. That is 240 searches worth a page. The old site had five pages. Nobody in the market was building a page for how a person in one town types one problem.

What We Did

  • We rebuilt the site so each service has a page in each city, with its own writing. More than 180 are live.
  • We built out the real estate inspection page, because home sales bring the best septic jobs and the deadline is short.
  • We set up Search Console and Analytics so the owner could see the numbers every night.

The Measured Result

On October 26, 2026, a search for emergency septic service near me from Cumming, Georgia showed the company at number 1 in the map pack.

Google showed the site 16,794 times from October 1 to 27, 2026. September had 3,463. That is close to five times as many.

Clicks are still small. October brought 22, up from 10 in September. The site now shows for far more searches, many of them on page two. Moving those up is the next job.

Organic search visits went from 14 between September 8 and 30 to 30 between October 1 and 28.

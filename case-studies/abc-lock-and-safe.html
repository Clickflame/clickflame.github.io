ABC Lock And Safe Case Study On A Record Month Of Calls

230Tracked Calls, October 1 To 30, 2026
176Same 30 Days In 2025
166First Time Callers

The Problem

ABC Lock and Safe has worked in Portland since 1985. The reputation was never the issue. The website did not show forty years of skill, and the search results were full of call centers with no shop in town.

A shop that picks a lock before it drills one should not lose to a lead seller. Online, it was.

What We Did

  • We built a fast, plain site that says who they are in the first screen.
  • We built nine location pages across the Portland area and a page for each of the four trades.
  • We gave safe work its own page, because few shops can open or move a safe and people search for it by name.
  • We put tracking numbers on the site so every call gets counted.

The Measured Result

The shop took 230 tracked calls from October 1 to 30, 2026. That is the most in any month since tracking began in September 2024.

The same 30 days in 2025 brought 176. Of the 230 calls, 204 were answered and 166 came from first time callers.

Search is still young here. The site went into Search Console on September 8, 2026, and Google clicks dipped from 47 to 37 while impressions rose to 4,461 from October 1 to 27. Calls are up first. Clicks are the next thing to fix.

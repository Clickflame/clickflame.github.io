How To Read Google Search Console In Twenty Minutes

Search Console is free and it comes from Google itself. Most owners open it once, see a wall of charts and close it. You only need four numbers.

Why It Matters

Moving a page from spot 8 to spot 3 is far easier than taking a page from nowhere to spot 10. Search Console tells you which pages are that close.

One warning. The data runs a few days behind, so do not judge yesterday.

How To Do It

  1. Open Performance. Turn on all four boxes at the top.
  2. Clicks are visits from Google. Impressions are how many times you were shown.
  3. Click rate is clicks divided by impressions. Average position is where you sat in the results.
  4. Open Queries and filter position between 4 and 15. These searches are on page one or just off it.
  5. Sort by impressions. High impressions with a middle position is your best chance. Fix those pages first.

Done when you have a short list of searches in positions 4 to 15, sorted by impressions.

One tip. Compare the same days a year apart when you can. Month against month can fool you in a seasonal trade.

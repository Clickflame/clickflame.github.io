How To Get More Google Reviews The Right Way

Review count, how recent they are and your rating all feed local ranking. They cost nothing. You only have to remember to ask.

Why It Matters

Most happy customers will leave a review if you make it one tap. Almost none will go looking for the form.

Timing matters more than wording. Ask right after the win, not in the invoice and not a week later.

How To Do It

  1. Get your direct review link. In your profile, choose Ask for reviews and copy the short link.
  2. Save it in a text template on the phone you use for work.
  3. Send it the day the job is done, while the customer is still happy.
  4. Reply to every review, good or bad.
  5. Never buy or fake a review. It can get your profile suspended.

Done when the link is saved in a template and has gone to every customer from the last 30 days.

One tip. Put a face on your website. People write reviews for people, not for logos.

Go Deeper

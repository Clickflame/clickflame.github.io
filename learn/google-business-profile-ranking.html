How Google Business Profile Ranking Works

We audit a lot of Google Business Profiles. The usual problem is not a trick someone missed. The profile is half empty.

Why It Matters

Google says it ranks local results on three things. How well you match the search, how close you are, and how well known you are. You can not move your building. You can fix the other two.

For our own repair shop, the profile is one of the biggest sources of calls. Many of those callers never open the website at all.

How To Do It

  1. Fill every field. Categories, services, products, hours, attributes and the description.
  2. Pick the most specific primary category you can. It is the strongest single choice on the profile.
  3. Add at least 10 real photos of your work, your team and your place. No stock.
  4. Verify the listing if you have not yet.
  5. Put customer questions and answers on your website. Google took the question section off profiles in late 2025 and now answers from your profile, reviews and site.

Done when every field is filled in and the profile shows at least 10 real photos.

One tip. Point the profile at your own website. The free sites Google used to build were retired in 2024. Google explains its local ranking in its own help pages.

How To Write Title Tags That Get The Click

The title tag is the blue headline people click in Google. It is also one of the strongest signals on the page. Most sites waste it on the word Home.

Why It Matters

A sharper title lifts ranking and clicks at once. It is free traffic from pages you already have.

Google cuts a title off at about 600 pixels wide, which is close to 60 characters.

How To Do It

  1. Lead with the main thing the page is about. The service, then the place.
  2. Keep it under about 60 characters so it is not cut off.
  3. Add a reason to click that a rival can not copy. A warranty, a real price or your coverage.
  4. Make every title on the site different.
  5. Keep it true to the page. Google rewrites titles that do not match.

Done when every page you care about has its own title, under 60 characters, that leads with what the page is about.

One tip. Put your phone number in the description under the title, not in the main heading on the page.

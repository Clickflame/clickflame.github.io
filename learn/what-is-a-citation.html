What A Citation Is And Which Ten To Claim First

A citation is your business name, address and phone number on someone else's website. Yelp is one. Apple Maps is one. So is the Better Business Bureau.

Why It Matters

Google checks your profile against these listings to confirm you are real and where you say you are. When they disagree, trust drops. Suite on one site and Ste on the next counts as a mismatch. So do two phone numbers.

When we audited about 45 listings for one restoration client, we found doubles and wrong numbers. That is normal. Almost every business has some.

How To Do It

  1. Lock one exact format for your name, address, phone and website. Write it in a sheet.
  2. Treat your Google Business Profile as the master copy. If your old website disagrees with it, find out which is right before you list anywhere.
  3. Claim the core ten first. Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business, Yelp, Facebook, Foursquare, Better Business Bureau, YP, Nextdoor and LinkedIn.
  4. Paste from the sheet every time. Never type it from memory.
  5. Add your logo, photos, hours and categories to each one, then verify it.

Done when all ten core listings are claimed and each link is saved in your sheet.

One tip. Do Google and Bing first. Their checks take the longest, and Bing can import your Google profile.

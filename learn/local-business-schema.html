How To Add Schema To A Local Business Website

Schema is a block of code that states the facts about your business so a machine does not have to guess. Name, address, hours, services and the area you cover.

Why It Matters

It helps search engines and AI tools read your page the right way. It is not magic and it will not rank a weak page.

Two things owners get wrong. Google will not show review stars that a business adds about itself. And schema that says something the page does not say gets ignored.

How To Do It

  1. Pick the most exact type for your trade. A roofer has its own type. So does a locksmith.
  2. Fill in name, address, phone, hours and service area to match your Google profile.
  3. List your profiles under sameAs. Google Business Profile, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and Yelp.
  4. Add a visible list of questions and answers to each service page, and mark it up word for word.
  5. Test it in the Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator. Fix every error.

Done when each page type passes both tests with no errors.

One tip. Only mark up what a visitor can see on the page. The Schema Markup Validator is free.

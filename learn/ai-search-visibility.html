How To Show Up In AI Search Like ChatGPT

People now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity who to call. Those tools can only name you if they are allowed to read your site. Many sites block them by accident.

Why It Matters

This traffic is small today and it is real. One investigator client got 46 visits from AI assistants between September 29 and October 28, 2026.

The gate is a tiny file named robots.txt at the root of your site. One wrong line in it can hide every page you have.

How To Do It

  1. Open yourdomain.com/robots.txt in a browser.
  2. Look for a line that says Disallow followed by a single slash. That blocks everything. Take it out unless the site is a test copy.
  3. Make sure the file lists your sitemap.
  4. Check that search crawlers are not blocked. That means Google, Bing, and the search crawlers from ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude.
  5. If your site runs through Cloudflare, check its AI crawler setting. New sites there have blocked AI crawlers by default since July 2025.
  6. Answer real customer questions on the page in plain words. AI tools quote clear answers.

Done when your robots.txt blocks no search crawler and lists your sitemap.

One tip. An llms.txt file is optional. No major search engine has said it uses one, so do not spend long on it.

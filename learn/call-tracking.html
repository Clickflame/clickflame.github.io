How To Track Calls So You Know What Works

A website that ranks and a phone that rings are two different things. Calls are the number that pays the bills, so count them.

Why It Matters

Without tracking you can not tell which channel is working. You can only guess. One bail bond client had decades in the trade and no idea which county or keyword paid for itself.

Once every channel had its own number, we could count 11,665 first time callers over 25 months and see where each one came from.

How To Do It

  1. Give each channel its own tracking number. Website, Google profile and ads at the least.
  2. Have each number ring your real line. Nothing changes for the customer.
  3. Count first time callers apart from repeat callers. New people are the growth.
  4. Count answered calls too. One locksmith we work with answered 204 of 230 calls in a month. The other 26 are money left on the table.
  5. Text back every missed call from the number the person dialed.

Done when every channel has its own number and you can say how many first time callers each one sent last month.

One tip. Keep your main number on your Google profile as an extra line if you swap in a tracking number, so your listings still match.

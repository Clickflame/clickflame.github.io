Who The Clean Editorial Style Suits

Clean Editorial suits practices that sell trust before anything else. Therapists, clinics, law offices and advisors. It leans on a text serif, wide margins and hairline rules, so the words do the work. It is a poor fit for an emergency trade where the phone number has to shout.

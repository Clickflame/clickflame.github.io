Who The Soft Pastel Style Suits

Soft Pastel suits sites people visit when they are worried. Health directories, child care, pet care, salons and community groups. Pale lilac, mint and peach with dark ink keep it kind and still easy to read. Every pastel here sits behind dark text, never under light text.

Who The Photo Led Magazine Style Suits

Photo Led Magazine suits owners who already take pictures of their work. Mobile mechanics, detailers, remodelers, roofers and food trucks. One big real photo leads and the words sit on a solid panel so they stay readable. Stock photos ruin it, so we do not use them.
